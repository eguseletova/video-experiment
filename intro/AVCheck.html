Camera & Microphone Check
Participation in this study requires a camera and microphone. Please test your camera and microphone before beginning.
  1. Click Test Camera & Microphone .
    Grant permission when your browser requests access.
  2. When your video appears, position yourself within the on-screen framing guide.
  3. Click Record 5-Second Test .
    Review the recording to confirm that both video and audio are working properly. You may record as many test videos as you like, to adjust your camera and microphone.
Privacy Note: Test recordings are not saved, stored, or viewed by the research team. They are automatically deleted when you leave this page.
⚠ If your camera or microphone does not work (Click to expand) ▼
  • Refresh this page and try again.
  • Check your browser settings to confirm camera and microphone access are enabled for this site.
  • Close other applications (e.g., Zoom, Teams) that may be using your camera or microphone.
If your camera and microphone cannot be accessed, you will not be able to participate in this study.
