Thank you for participating in this study! We’re exploring decision-making in the job application process. This study will take about 15 to 20 minutes to complete.
Your Role
Today, you will take on the role of a Job Candidate.
Your Task

This study consists of two stages.

  1. In Stage 1, you will complete a series of short screening tasks designed to assess your skills.

  2. In Stage 2, you will make some decisions related to the tasks you completed.

Payment
You will receive a $3.00 participation payment for fully completing the study.
In addition, your decisions in today’s study may affect your bonus payment. One part of the study (the “part-that-counts”) will be randomly selected for bonus payment. 
  • We will clearly indicate when a part may count towards your bonus and provide details at that time.
  • Because you won’t know in advance which part will count, please treat each decision as if it could affect your bonus.
