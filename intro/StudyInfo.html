Important Information About This Study
This study includes recording a short video message. Your recording may be viewed by other study participants in future related studies and in academic presentations.

By continuing, you agree to: (a) record a short video as part of this study, (b) allow the research team to store and retain your recording for research purposes, and (c) allow your video to be viewed by other participants in future related studies and in academic presentations.
⚠ Before continuing, please confirm the following.
  • You are using Chrome or Firefox on a desktop or laptop computer (Safari and mobile browsers may not work properly).
  • You have a working camera and microphone.
  • You are in a private space so no one else is inadvertently recorded.
Your participation is voluntary. If you do not wish to record a video, you may choose not to continue.
