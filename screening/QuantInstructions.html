Screener 1 out of 3: Quantitative Analysis Task

The goal of this task is to try to find the number (between 0 and 300) that results in your computer returning the biggest possible value. You will have {{ max_guesses }} guesses. A guess can be any number between 0 and 300.

After you enter your guess, the computer will give you back a number. There is a systematic relationship between the number you guess, and the number you receive, but the relationship may be difficult to understand. Usually, numbers that are close to each other will receive very similar outputs.

After your {{ max_guesses }} guesses, you will be asked to enter the number that you believe gives the highest response. It is only this final answer that determines your score!
