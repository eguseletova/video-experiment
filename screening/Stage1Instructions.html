Stage 1: Screening

In Stage 1, you will complete a series of screening tasks designed to assess a range of relevant skills. This stage will take around 10 to 15 minutes to complete.

 

Specifically, you will complete:

  • An Error Detection Task, which assesses your attention to detail; 

  • A Quantitative Analysis Task, which assesses your quantitative and analytical skills; and

  • A Research Communication Task, which assesses your ability to understand and communicate written information clearly.
     

No cheating is allowed on these screeners. You may not use any outside tools or assistance, including calculators, search engines, AI tools (such as ChatGPT), or help from other people. Detection of cheating or failure to follow these instructions will result in forfeiture of any bonus payment.
