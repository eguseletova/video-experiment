Sending an Additional Video

You chose to send Video {{ sent_upper }} with your application.

Now imagine you could send the Recruiter one extra video as well, but it costs money: the price would be taken out of your bonus.

Below are different prices. For each price, tell us: would you pay this price to send an extra video?

The simplest way to answer: think about the most you would be willing to pay. Choose "Yes" for every price up to that amount, and "No" for every price above it.

Example: if the most you would pay is $0.30, choose "Yes" for $0.00 to $0.30 and "No" for $0.40 and above. Clicking one row fills in the rest for you; you can still change rows.

How this affects your bonus

If this part is selected for your bonus, the computer randomly picks one of the prices below. If you said "Yes" to that price, it is taken out of your bonus and your extra video is sent to the Recruiter. If you said "No", nothing is taken out and only Video {{ sent_upper }} is sent. Because you don't know which price will be picked, the best strategy is to answer every row honestly.
