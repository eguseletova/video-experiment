Job Posting

Role: Research Assistant

We are seeking candidates for a short-term Research Assistant role. In this position, you would support a research team by helping complete routine project tasks.

Tasks may include:

  • Working with spreadsheets or research data (for example, data entry or coding)
  • Reviewing short scientific articles and clearly summarizing their key findings
  • Proofreading documents for errors, missing information, or inconsistencies

Qualifications:

This position does not require prior research experience. However, the role primarily involves quantitative analysis work (approximately 75%), along with research communication (20%) and attention to detail (5%).

Your Potential Bonus Earnings
If this part is selected to determine your bonus payment:
  • If a Recruiter recommends you for the position, you will earn an additional $1, on top of your participation payment.
  • If you are not recommended, you will earn no additional bonus on top of your participation payment.
