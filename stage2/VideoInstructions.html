Stage 2: Resume

Many hiring processes use brief video introductions as part of their screening process to learn more about candidates and give them an opportunity to share additional information beyond what is shown in their resume.

Your Task
In addition to your resume, you will be asked to record short video introductions.

In your videos, you may share any additional information you believe is relevant for the role. Your video introduction will be shown to the Recruiter, along with the other information in your resume.

Video Recording Instructions
  • You will record three short videos (Video A, Video B, and Video C). Each video responds to a different prompt.
  • Each video can be up to 1 minute long.
  • You may re-record each video up to 2 times. Your last recording of each video is the one that counts.
  • Your background will be blurred automatically during recording.
Important Privacy Rules
  • Do not include identifying or sensitive personal information in your recording about yourself or others, such as your full name, contact information, or age.
  • If you would like to introduce yourself by name, you may use an alias (a made-up name) instead of your real name.
  • Any audio recording should be made only in a private space so that no passerby is inadvertently recorded.
  • Failure to follow these guidelines may result in forfeiture of any potential bonus in the study.
